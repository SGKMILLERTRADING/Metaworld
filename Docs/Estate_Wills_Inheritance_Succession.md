# Metaworld — Wills, Beneficiaries, Family, Succession & Estates

## Canonical Rule

This system extends Metaworld's permanent-death rules without weakening them.

When a character dies permanently:

- The character identity is dead.
- The account survives and later creates a new character identity/name.
- No second new-player food/water package is granted.
- Physical possessions remain in the persistent world.
- Killers and robbers can only take what they can physically reach: carried GrimKoin, carried items, and property/items they successfully breach or seize in houses, vehicles, businesses, warehouses, storage, etc.
- No killer, robber, hostile player, or hostile NPC receives access to the deceased player's bank account.
- The bank may keep up to 50% of banked GrimKoin through the separate bank death/estate settlement rule.
- The surviving bank balance enters estate processing.
- Tracking codes help locate eligible former assets but do not teleport or restore them.
- Legacy/recovery rights expire after the configured recovery period.

Core principle:

> Inheritance gives legal rights and claims. It does not magically move physical property out of the living world.

---

# 1. Wills

A player can create and register a will while their character is alive.

The will is a server-authoritative legal record linked to that character's identity.

A will can name:

- Executor/estate administrator
- Beneficiaries
- Backup beneficiaries
- Spouse/family allocations
- Specific property
- Specific vehicles
- Specific containers or valuable items
- Business ownership shares
- Bank-estate distribution percentages
- Instructions to sell, keep, divide, or continue a business

The latest valid registered will normally supersedes older versions.

A will cannot:

- Give away property the character did not own
- Bypass the bank's 0–50% death settlement
- Bypass valid city taxes or debts
- Mint GrimKoin
- Teleport stolen or occupied property to a beneficiary
- Reveal tracking codes the player never saved
- Make the player's future new character automatically inherit everything

## Tracking Codes in a Will

If a player wants beneficiaries to receive asset tracking/recovery codes after death, those codes must be deliberately stored in an approved estate record, will attachment, bank safe-deposit record, or similar secure legacy system while alive.

If the player never saved the tracking code, the estate cannot invent it after death.

---

# 2. Beneficiaries

A beneficiary is a valid player, NPC, organization, business, or other legal entity named to receive part of an estate.

Possible beneficiaries include:

- Spouse
- Family
- Friends
- Business partners
- Employees
- Player organizations/factions where allowed
- Businesses
- Community/religious organizations later

A beneficiary can receive:

- A percentage of surviving estate GrimKoin
- Legal ownership shares
- Property title/claim
- Business shares
- Specific vehicles/items
- Rights defined in the will

## Backup Beneficiaries

A will should allow backups.

If the first beneficiary is dead, disqualified, or unable to claim, the next valid beneficiary can inherit.

If nobody valid remains, the asset enters default succession or unclaimed-estate processing.

## No Automatic Future-Self Beneficiary

A character cannot simply write a will saying "give everything to my next character" and erase the consequence of permanent death.

The continuing account may later qualify for limited residual legacy claims under Metaworld's estate rules, but only after the will, spouse/family, taxes, debts, disputes, and other valid claims have been processed.

---

# 3. Spouses and Family

Marriage and family relationships can have real legal and economic meaning in Metaworld.

Family records can include:

- Spouse/marriage status
- Registered dependents/children later
- Household membership
- Next-of-kin
- Shared property arrangements
- Shared business ownership
- Named family beneficiaries

## With a Will

A valid will controls distribution unless city law gives a protected minimum share to a spouse/family member.

## Without a Will

The city uses a default inheritance order.

Example configurable order:

1. Surviving spouse
2. Registered children/dependents/family
3. Other next-of-kin
4. Valid business co-owners for business-specific shares
5. Eligible residual legacy claim
6. Unclaimed estate

Different cities may eventually have different inheritance laws, but those rules must be published clearly.

## Killer Cannot Automatically Inherit

A city can enforce a rule that a beneficiary convicted of intentionally killing the deceased cannot inherit from that estate.

That becomes a court/legal rule, not an invisible game trick.

---

# 4. Business Ownership Succession

A business has its own persistent Business ID and does not disappear because the owner dies.

The business may continue to have:

- Employees
- Payroll
- Rent
- Utilities
- Taxes
- Inventory
- Security contracts
- Billboard/advertising contracts
- Customer orders
- Property leases
- Debts

A will can specify:

- Who inherits the business
- Who receives ownership percentages
- Whether a spouse/family member takes control
- Whether a partner becomes controlling owner
- Whether an executor manages it temporarily
- Whether it should be sold or auctioned
- Whether it should be closed/liquidated

If a player owned only 40% of a company, only that 40% enters the estate.

## Interim Management

While succession is being processed, the business can be run by:

- Existing co-owner
- Registered manager
- Executor
- Court-appointed administrator
- Qualified NPC manager

Bills do not pause because the owner died.

Payroll, rent, taxes, utilities, and security still come due on the real Metaworld calendar.

## Ownership Does Not Grant Profession Skill

A spouse can inherit a gunsmith shop without becoming a gunsmith.

They may have to:

- Keep or hire a qualified gunsmith
- Learn the profession
- Sell the business
- Change the business if city rules allow

Ownership and professional capability stay separate.

---

# 5. Inheritance Taxes

Inheritance tax is a city tax.

It is separate from:

- Metaworld marketplace commissions
- Ordinary sales tax
- The bank's 0–50% death settlement

Possible taxable inherited value:

- Surviving bank-estate GrimKoin
- Property
- Business shares
- Vehicles
- High-value assets
- Rental portfolios

Possible city-specific exemptions/reductions can later exist for:

- Spouses
- Close family
- Small estates
- Certain business-continuity cases

## Recommended Estate Order

1. Determine banked GrimKoin at death.
2. Bank applies its configured death settlement, maximum 50%.
3. Remaining banked GrimKoin becomes estate funds.
4. Process valid debts and estate obligations.
5. Calculate inheritance/estate taxes.
6. Reserve disputed assets/funds if court cases exist.
7. Distribute remaining legal shares/claims to beneficiaries.

The bank's death share becomes bank revenue.

Inheritance tax goes to the city treasury and helps fund city workers/services.

---

# 6. Court Disputes

Estate disputes create real work for:

- Lawyers
- Judges
- Court workers
- Investigators
- Police/bailiffs
- Business partners
- Families

Possible disputes include:

- Competing wills
- Old will vs newer will
- Disputed spouse/family status
- Disputed business ownership
- Disputed property title
- Debt claims
- Executor misconduct
- Theft before death
- Beneficiary accused/convicted of killing the deceased
- Current physical possessor refusing to return legally inherited property

Registered wills and estate records should be server-signed/versioned so players cannot forge them by editing local files.

A court may:

- Confirm the valid will
- Reject an invalid/superseded will
- Determine beneficiary order
- Freeze disputed estate funds
- Freeze business-share transfer
- Award legal title
- Order repayment
- Order property return
- Appoint an administrator
- Resolve business succession

A court ruling does not teleport physical property.

If a hostile group physically holds the inherited truck, police, security, bailiffs, friends, or the beneficiary still have to recover it in the world.

---

# 7. Unclaimed Estates

An estate becomes unclaimed when no eligible beneficiary or claimant successfully completes the claim process before the applicable deadline.

## Unclaimed Banked GrimKoin

After:

- Bank death settlement
- Debts
- Taxes
- Court holds
- Claim deadline

remaining unclaimed estate GrimKoin can pass according to city/bank rules, such as:

- Bank estate revenue/pool
- City treasury
- Other configured public estate destination

Every transfer must be ledger-recorded.

## Physical Assets

If another player or NPC successfully possesses and claims an asset under world rules before recovery/estate rights expire, that current possessor may keep it.

Abandoned physical assets can move into:

- City auction
- Repossession
- Salvage
- Foreclosure
- NPC ownership
- Abandoned-property claims
- Demolition/cleanup where appropriate

## Unclaimed Businesses

A business may be:

- Auctioned
- Purchased by a partner
- Taken through creditor rules
- Temporarily administered by city/NPC systems
- Liquidated
- Closed

Nothing should simply vanish without an economic/world reason.

---

# 8. Bank Inheritance Claims

No player has automatic access to another player's bank account.

This includes:

- Killers
- Robbers
- Friends
- Spouses
- Family
- Beneficiaries
- Business partners
- The new character created by the same account

After permanent death, the bank handles the deceased account through estate processing.

Flow:

Banked GrimKoin
-> Bank keeps configured death share, maximum 50%
-> Remaining balance becomes estate funds
-> Debts/taxes/court holds processed
-> Valid inheritance claims processed
-> Approved amounts paid to beneficiaries

A claimant must have a legitimate server-recognized basis such as:

- Named beneficiary in a valid will
- Surviving spouse/family status
- Business succession right
- Executor authority
- Court judgment
- Eligible residual legacy claim

The claimant does not log into the deceased person's bank account.

The bank transfers the legally approved amount from the estate account to the claimant's own bank/wallet according to the settlement rules.

## Continuing Account / New Character

The player's new character may later submit an eligible residual legacy claim for estate funds not validly assigned elsewhere.

It is not automatic.

It occurs after:

- Bank death settlement
- Will/beneficiary priority
- Spouse/family rights
- Debts
- Inheritance tax
- Court disputes/holds

This keeps death costly while still allowing a structured legacy system.

# Estate Example

Character Marcus has:

- 40,000 banked GrimKoin
- A house
- A nightclub
- A truck
- A spouse
- Two friends
- A registered will
- A saved truck tracking code

Marcus dies.

1. Marcus is permanently dead.
2. The killer can loot only physical items Marcus carried and anything they later successfully raid in the physical world.
3. The killer has zero access to Marcus's bank account.
4. The bank applies its death settlement to the 40,000 banked GrimKoin, up to the 50% maximum.
5. The surviving amount enters the estate.
6. Debts and inheritance taxes are processed.
7. The spouse receives the will-defined bank-estate share through a bank inheritance claim.
8. The nightclub passes according to the business succession plan.
9. The truck is legally left to the spouse, but raiders have physically stolen it.
10. The spouse/friends use the stored tracking code during the recovery window.
11. They still have to recover the truck through police, negotiation, payment, theft-back, or combat under normal world rules.
12. If recovery rights expire before the truck is reclaimed and the raiders successfully hold/claim it, the truck is gone from the estate.

The will organizes what should happen. It does not make death safe.

---

# Core Principle

Metaworld keeps all eight systems:

1. Wills
2. Beneficiaries
3. Spouses/family
4. Business ownership succession
5. Inheritance taxes
6. Court disputes
7. Unclaimed estates
8. Bank inheritance claims

They work together without changing the harsh permanent-death rules.

The bank protects account privacy from killers and robbers, but the bank itself profits from the death settlement.

Physical property stays physical.

Legal ownership and physical possession can become different things, and the player world has to resolve the conflict.
