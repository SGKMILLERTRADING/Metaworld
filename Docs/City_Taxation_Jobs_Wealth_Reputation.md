# Metaworld — City Taxation, Jobs, Public Payroll & Economic Reputation

## Canonical Principle

Metaworld cities are not decorative service providers with infinite money.

A functioning city collects taxes from economic activity and uses that revenue to pay public workers, maintain services, fund infrastructure, respond to emergencies, and keep the city operating.

Private businesses are responsible for their own employees and private security.

Money also has a social signal. How a player earns, spends, displays, and circulates GrimKoin and PromoKoin can affect how NPCs, businesses, criminals, officials, and other players perceive that person.

Core rule:

> Money buys things, but money also tells the world something about you.

---

# 1. City Treasury

Every city/municipality should have a persistent treasury rather than unlimited abstract funding.

The treasury can track:

- Current balance
- Tax revenue by category
- Public payroll obligations
- Infrastructure spending
- Emergency spending
- Public contracts
- Debt/shortfall if that system is enabled later
- Reserve/emergency fund
- Historical income and expenses

Public finance should be server-authoritative and auditable.

The city does not receive Metaworld's platform/marketplace commission. Platform revenue and city tax are separate systems.

---

# 2. What the City Can Tax

Taxable economic activity can include, subject to configurable local rules:

- Retail sales
- Player shop sales
- Creator-product sales
- Business sales
- Food/drink sales
- Service payments
- Room rentals
- Apartment rentals
- House rentals
- Commercial leases
- Property sales
- Vehicle sales
- Entertainment tickets
- Drive-in/cinema admissions
- Club entry fees
- Music/media sales where applicable
- Business profits/revenue where enabled
- Property ownership/land taxes where enabled
- Licensing/permit fees
- Market stall/vendor fees
- Other city-regulated transactions

Not every city needs identical rates. Tax policy can become part of local government, elections, economics, and player politics later.

Taxes should normally be collected automatically at the transaction layer so businesses do not manually calculate every payment.

Example:

Customer pays 100 GrimKoin
-> Transaction service calculates applicable city tax
-> Business receives net business proceeds
-> City treasury receives tax share
-> Metaworld platform commission is handled separately if the transaction category has one
-> Full ledger record is created

---

# 3. City Workers Are Paid From Tax Revenue

Public workers can include:

- Police
- Firefighters
- Emergency responders
- City administrators
- Court workers
- Public defenders/prosecutors where applicable
- Sanitation workers
- Road/maintenance crews
- Utility workers if publicly operated
- Public transit workers
- Inspectors
- Public hospital/clinic workers where applicable
- Teachers/trainers if public institutions are added
- Other municipal employees

Their wages come from the city treasury.

If a city is prosperous and well-managed, it can afford:

- More police patrols
- Better emergency response
- Better lighting
- Better roads
- More public workers
- Faster repairs
- Better public transportation
- More public facilities
- Better wages
- Better disaster preparation

If a city is poorly funded or badly managed, consequences can include:

- Fewer patrols
- Slower emergency response
- Unrepaired infrastructure
- Street lights remaining broken
- Lower public-worker wages
- Worker shortages
- Public-service interruptions
- Greater crime opportunity
- Lower city reputation
- Political pressure

This makes taxation meaningful rather than decorative.

---

# 4. Private Security Is Paid Privately

Police are public workers funded by the city.

Private security is not.

Businesses and property owners pay for their own:

- Guards
- Bouncers
- Bodyguards
- Event security
- Convoy escorts
- Property patrols
- Security managers
- Hired NPC guards
- Player security companies

Examples:

A nightclub owner pays the bouncers/security.

A mansion owner pays private guards.

A drive-in owner pays event security.

A warehouse owner pays guards to protect inventory.

A landlord may include security costs in rent or pay for a building security contract.

Police may still respond to crimes, but private security exists to protect the owner's peace before or while police arrive.

---

# 5. Private Businesses Pay Their Own Employees

Business owners pay their workers from business revenue.

Examples:

- Bar owner pays bartenders
- Restaurant owner pays cooks and servers
- Garage owner pays mechanics
- Club owner pays DJs, bartenders, dancers/entertainers, cleaners and security
- Store owner pays clerks
- Farm owner pays farmhands
- Builder/company owner pays builders
- Transport company pays drivers
- Hospital owner pays private medical staff if privately operated
- Cinema/drive-in owner pays attendants, projection/media staff, cleaners and security

Business payroll should be a real expense.

A badly run business can fail if revenue cannot cover:

- Wages
- Rent
- Taxes
- Inventory
- Utilities
- Repairs
- Security
- Other operating costs

---

# 6. Tips

Workers in appropriate professions can receive tips directly from customers.

Examples:

- Bartenders
- Servers
- DJs/performers
- Musicians
- Drivers
- Delivery workers
- Hotel/room-service workers
- Other service roles

A bartender can therefore earn:

Base wage from bar owner
+
Customer tips
=
Total income

Tips should normally belong to the worker unless a business explicitly uses a clearly disclosed tip-pool policy.

Tipping can affect relationships/reputation:

- Generous customer
- Cheap customer
- Regular customer
- Favorite client

But tipping should remain voluntary unless a venue clearly charges an automatic service fee.

---

# 7. There Should Always Be Work Available

Metaworld should avoid the situation where a new/broke player has literally no legal way to earn GrimKoin.

The economy should maintain a range of jobs, contracts, gigs, and public work opportunities.

Examples:

- Cleaning
- Deliveries
- Loading/unloading
- Farm work
- Construction labor
- Security work
- Driving
- Repair assistance
- Sanitation
- Public maintenance
- Store work
- Bar/restaurant work
- Scavenging contracts
- Courier work
- Emergency recovery work
- Temporary city jobs
- Player-posted jobs
- NPC/business-posted jobs

Higher-skill professions pay more, but low-entry work should generally exist so a player can work their way upward.

---

# 8. Economic Reputation / Financial Social Signal

Metaworld should not treat currency usage as invisible information.

Spending patterns can generate a social/economic reputation that affects how people perceive the player.

Possible reputation dimensions:

- Poor / Struggling
- Ordinary / Working
- Frugal
- Dependent / Low-Contribution
- Comfortable
- Wealthy
- Very Wealthy
- High Roller
- Business Elite
- Financially Reckless
- Suspicious Wealth
- Generous
- Cheap
- Reliable Payer
- Employer
- Tax Contributor
- Major Local Investor

These are not rigid permanent labels. They evolve from observable behavior.

---

# 9. Heavy PromoKoin Spending Signal

PromoKoin is easier to acquire and has limited purchasing power.

If a player persistently spends mostly PromoKoin while contributing very little through work, business, GrimKoin commerce, taxes, or productive activity, some NPCs/institutions may begin to perceive that player as:

- Low-income
- Dependent
- Unambitious
- Avoiding work
- Low economic contributor

This should be a social inference, not magical truth.

The game should consider context such as:

- Employment history
- Available work opportunities
- Tax contribution
- Business ownership
- Recent hardship
- Injuries/disability/status where applicable
- Criminal income where known
- Public assistance systems if ever introduced

Spending PromoKoin is not itself a crime and should never automatically trigger punishment.

The point is that a long-term economic pattern affects reputation.

---

# 10. Heavy GrimKoin Spending Signal

A player who publicly spends large amounts of GrimKoin signals wealth.

Visible high-value behavior can include:

- Buying expensive vehicles
- Buying property
- Purchasing high-end creator goods
- Paying large tabs
- Hosting expensive events
- Hiring large teams
- Large public tips
- Buying premium equipment
- Large business investments

As the player's visible wealth reputation rises, reactions can include:

Positive:

- VIP treatment
- Better business opportunities
- Invitations
- Access to wealthy social circles
- Sellers seeking the player's business
- Partnership offers
- Political influence/reputation
- Better credit/trust systems if later implemented

Negative:

- Merchants testing higher prices where dynamic negotiation is allowed
- Scammers targeting the player
- Thieves targeting the player
- Criminal groups noticing the player
- Kidnapping/extortion risk where appropriate to game rules
- Rivals watching the player
- Gossip spreading
- Greater public attention

Core rule:

> Wealth creates opportunity, but visible wealth also creates risk.

---

# 11. Balanced Spending / Low-Profile Wealth

A player who mixes GrimKoin and PromoKoin naturally and does not constantly display extreme wealth may be perceived as more ordinary or difficult to classify.

Example:

- Uses PromoKoin for small everyday items
- Uses GrimKoin when required for meaningful purchases
- Works or operates a business
- Pays taxes
- Does not constantly make extravagant purchases

NPCs may read this person as:

- Working class
- Middle class
- Ordinary
- Frugal
- Low-profile
- Possibly poor
- Possibly quietly wealthy

This lets players deliberately manage their public image.

A wealthy player can choose not to advertise wealth.

---

# 12. No Omniscient NPC Knowledge

NPCs should not magically know a player's exact bank balance.

Economic reputation should be built from information that can reasonably become known, such as:

- Public purchases
- Property ownership records
- Business ownership
- Visible vehicles/clothing/assets
- Public transactions
- City tax/payment records available to authorized institutions
- Employment
- Public tips
- News coverage
- Gossip
- Witnessed spending
- Known debts or failures
- Criminal intelligence

A shopkeeper who has never met the player should not automatically know the player's exact GrimKoin balance.

This keeps the system believable.

---

# 13. Wealth Visibility / Target-on-Back System

Metaworld can maintain a hidden/server-side Wealth Visibility score derived from observable wealth signals.

Possible contributors:

- High-value purchases
- Expensive property
- Luxury vehicles
- Large public tips
- Large payroll/business ownership
- News coverage
- Public auctions
- Expensive clothing/items
- Known large transactions

The score can influence which NPCs or systems notice the player.

Low visibility:

- Ordinary NPC attention
- Low criminal interest
- Few elite invitations

High visibility:

- Elite social invitations
- Investor/business opportunities
- Political attention
- Criminal surveillance
- Robbery/extortion plans
- Rival attention
- News interest
- Reputation growth

This creates the player's 'target on your back' without simply spawning attackers because the wallet number crossed a threshold.

---

# 14. Dynamic Pricing Must Be Bounded

Some independent merchants or player sellers may charge more when they believe a buyer is rich, especially in negotiable markets.

However, Metaworld should avoid secretly multiplying every price across the entire world just because someone is wealthy.

Use bounded systems such as:

- Negotiation
- Seller personality
- Reputation
- Luxury pricing
- Dynamic offers
- Player-set prices
- Scarcity
- Local demand

Government-regulated prices or fixed-price vendors can remain stable where appropriate.

This preserves the user's idea that visible wealth can make things more expensive without making the economy feel rigged.

---

# 15. Tax Reputation

Tax behavior can create its own reputation.

Possible states:

- Regular taxpayer
- Major contributor
- Business contributor
- Delinquent
- Under investigation
- Tax evader if illegal evasion systems are added

Major legitimate contributors may gain:

- Business reputation
- Invitations to civic events
- Political attention
- Recognition
- Better relationship with city institutions

But public workers must still enforce the law rather than becoming personally owned by rich taxpayers.

---

# 16. City Budget Creates Gameplay

Example prosperous city loop:

Businesses sell goods
-> Rent is paid
-> Players work
-> Taxes enter city treasury
-> City pays police/fire/maintenance
-> City is safer and cleaner
-> More businesses open
-> More players move in
-> More commerce occurs
-> Treasury grows

Example failing city loop:

Businesses close
-> Tax revenue falls
-> City cuts services
-> Police presence falls
-> Crime rises
-> Property values fall
-> Wealthy players leave
-> Revenue falls further

Players/government can then try to reverse the decline.

---

# 17. Public Contracts

The city does not need NPC workers for every task.

It can contract player businesses for:

- Road repairs
- Building construction
- Cleanup
- Security support
- Vehicle towing
- Medical supply delivery
- Food supply
- Emergency rebuilding
- Utility repairs
- Snow/storm cleanup
- Other municipal work

This creates additional GrimKoin jobs and lets the city treasury feed money back into the player economy.

---

# 18. Important Economic Separation

Metaworld should keep these flows distinct:

## Platform Revenue

Metaworld marketplace commissions/base-asset revenue.

Purpose: supports the game/platform/business.

## City Taxes

Money collected by a city from economic activity.

Purpose: city payroll, public services, infrastructure, emergency response, public contracts.

## Private Business Revenue

Money earned by player/NPC businesses.

Purpose: wages, inventory, rent, security, utilities, profit.

## Tips

Direct voluntary payments from customers to workers/performers.

This separation makes the economy understandable and auditable.

---

# 19. Core Principle

Metaworld should always provide ways to earn GrimKoin through work, skill, trade, business, service, risk, or creativity.

Taxes keep cities functioning.

Cities pay public workers.

Businesses pay private workers.

Business/property owners pay private security.

Service workers can earn tips.

PromoKoin-heavy behavior can signal low contribution or dependence when supported by a larger pattern.

Heavy visible GrimKoin spending can signal wealth, raise status, unlock opportunity, attract higher prices in negotiable contexts, and place a target on the player's back.

Balanced spending can help a player blend in.

The world should react not only to how much money the player has, but to what other people can reasonably observe about how that player lives.
