# Metaworld — World Rank Hierarchy, Roleplay & Authority

**Status:** Canonical / Approved

## Canonical Rule

Metaworld uses an explicit **in-world player/NPC rank hierarchy** so players, NPCs, governments, departments, factions, businesses, and roleplay systems understand who outranks whom without confusing wealth, profession, job title, political office, platform administration, or real-world project ownership.

Core rules:

> There is **no Owner/Creator rank inside the playable Metaworld hierarchy**.

> The creator of Metaworld plays under the same in-world progression, survival, economy, voting, reputation, profession, law, death and rank rules as every other player.

> King / Sovereign Queen is the highest in-world player-held roleplay rank.

> President is directly below the Royal/Sovereign tier.

> Every official role has responsibilities and recurring duties appropriate to that role. Higher rank means greater responsibility, not free authority.

GrimKoin cannot directly buy official rank.

---

# 1. No Owner Rank in the Game

The real-world creator/operator status of Metaworld is **not represented as an in-world rank**.

There is no:

- `Rank.Owner`
- Owner badge
- Creator crown
- automatic ruler status
- special in-world political authority
- free profession qualification
- automatic access to private player property/banks
- automatic immunity
- automatic King/President status

When the creator enters Metaworld as a player character, that character starts and progresses under ordinary gameplay rules.

If the creator wants to become:

- Builder
- Police Officer
- Captain
- Mayor
- Governor
- President
- King
- Vampire leader
- Werewolf pack leader
- Business owner
- any other normal role

that character must qualify, work, gain reputation, win required votes/appointments, earn resources and survive exactly as the corresponding gameplay system requires.

Platform administration/moderation powers, if ever needed operationally, remain completely separate from visible roleplay rank and are not an in-world advantage.

Canonical playable hierarchy therefore begins with the highest earnable in-world rank: **King / Sovereign Queen**.

---

# 2. Tier 1 — King / Sovereign Royal Tier

**King is the highest normal in-world player-held rank.**

A King represents the highest sovereign leader recognized by the Metaworld roleplay hierarchy where that government uses a monarchy/royal structure.

King is not purchased.

A player reaches this tier only through the approved world/community process, including eligibility, reputation, political/community support and the required vote/selection rules.

Possible authority, depending on the kingdom/government structure, can include:

- sovereign leadership
- national/kingdom policy
- high-level appointments
- diplomacy
- war/peace authority where the constitution/rules permit
- oversight of subordinate governors/mayors/departments where applicable
- ceremonial/state leadership
- kingdom treasury/policy authority only through legitimate government systems

A King remains subject to Metaworld's world rules, law, evidence, economy, needs, reputation, permanent death, removal and succession rules.

## Queen Rule

Queen belongs to the same **Royal/Sovereign tier** when the Queen is the actual sovereign ruler.

A spouse using the title Queen does not automatically receive every governing permission of the King merely through marriage.

Metaworld separates:

- **Sovereign Queen** — Tier 1 governing authority equivalent to sovereign King where supported.
- **Royal Consort Queen** — royal/social title whose actual permissions come from the kingdom's laws, appointments and role definitions rather than automatic sovereign power.

The same distinction can apply to other royal spouses/consorts.

---

# 3. Tier 2 — President

**President is directly below the King/Royal Sovereign tier in the canonical global Metaworld roleplay hierarchy.**

President is an extremely high player-held civic rank and must be earned through the approved community election/selection process.

Possible President authority can include:

- national/republic leadership
- executive administration
- cabinet/department appointments where allowed
- national budget/policy proposals
- diplomacy
- emergency decisions
- military/security direction where constitutionally allowed
- coordination of governors, mayors, departments and public services

President does not outrank a sovereign King in the canonical global hierarchy when both titles coexist in the same authority structure.

A President cannot purchase the office with GrimKoin.

---

# 4. Recommended Global Civic Rank Ladder

Default broad hierarchy:

1. **King / Sovereign Queen** — highest in-world player-held rank
2. **President**
3. **Governor / Regional Leader**
4. **Mayor / City Leader**
5. **Council / Senior Civic Leadership**
6. **Department Heads / Chiefs / Commissioners**
7. **Senior Professional Command**
8. **Professional Officers / Workers / Licensed Specialists**
9. **Citizens / Residents / Civilians**

This is the global roleplay order, not a requirement that every government contains every title.

Examples:

Kingdom:

`King -> Governor -> Mayor -> Department Heads -> Citizens`

Republic:

`President -> Governor -> Mayor -> Department Heads -> Citizens`

Local territory:

`Governor -> Mayor -> Department Heads -> Citizens`

The hierarchy defines relative placement when titles coexist; it does not force every settlement to spawn every office.

---

# 5. Professional Ranks Sit Inside Their Organization

Professional ranks are scoped to the organization.

Example police hierarchy:

`Police Chief / Commissioner`
-> `Captain`
-> `Lieutenant`
-> `Sergeant`
-> `Senior Officer`
-> `Officer`

A Police Captain can command subordinate police within lawful department authority, but that does not make the Captain a higher civic authority than a Mayor, Governor, President or King.

Similarly:

- Chief Doctor manages appropriate hospital/medical operations, not the city.
- Business CEO manages the company, not public government.
- Guild leader controls the guild according to guild rules.
- Military commander commands assigned military units according to lawful structure.

Authority is **scoped** to jurisdiction and role.

---

# 6. Every Role Has Duties

An official Metaworld role is not merely a title displayed over a character's head.

Each profession, job, command role and civic office can have a **Role Duty Definition** describing the work/responsibility expected from that role.

Examples:

## Police Officer

Possible daily/shift duties:

- patrol assigned districts
- respond to calls
- investigate crimes
- collect/review evidence
- traffic/public-safety work
- guard events
- assist during disasters
- make lawful arrests where evidence permits
- write/close reports
- help citizens

## Police Captain

Possible duties:

- assign patrol coverage
- supervise officers
- review serious cases
- handle staffing problems
- coordinate emergencies
- review complaints/discipline
- communicate with city leadership
- maintain department readiness

## Builder

Possible duties/jobs:

- inspect build requests
- build foundations/walls/structures
- repair damaged property
- complete contracts
- follow permits/zoning
- manage materials
- respond to public reconstruction contracts

## Doctor / Medical Staff

Possible duties:

- treat patients
- respond to emergencies
- manage beds/resources
- diagnose permitted conditions
- stabilize injured characters
- maintain medical supplies

## Mayor

Possible duties:

- review city budget
- respond to public-service failures
- approve/deny configured contracts
- address infrastructure issues
- coordinate police/fire/public services
- respond to complaints
- manage local emergencies
- communicate policy to citizens

## Governor

Possible duties:

- coordinate multiple cities/regions
- manage regional budgets/policy
- resolve disputes between cities
- oversee regional emergencies
- coordinate transport/supply/security

## President

Possible duties:

- national policy and budget decisions
- appoint/coordinate high officials where allowed
- diplomacy
- national emergencies
- war/peace/security decisions within lawful authority
- respond to shortages/economic crises
- address regional disputes
- public communication

## King / Sovereign Queen

Possible duties:

- highest sovereign responsibilities
- diplomacy and treaty decisions
- major appointments where lawful
- kingdom-wide policy
- war/peace decisions where permitted
- resolve major political disputes
- supervise regional leadership structures
- respond to kingdom crises
- represent the realm publicly

Higher rank therefore creates **more responsibility**, not a free power bonus.

# 7. Dynamic Daily Task System

Role duties should be generated from the actual living world rather than repetitive fixed chores.

Bad design:

`Police daily quest: arrest 3 people.`

Better Metaworld design:

`City has robbery reports + storm damage + nightclub event tonight`

-> police duty board generates patrol/investigation/event-security work.

Tasks can be influenced by:

- role/profession
- rank
- jurisdiction
- current city/region
- time/shift
- world events
- crime level
- emergencies
- business demand
- staffing shortages
- weather
- war
- infrastructure failures
- player/NPC requests
- public contracts
- government policy

Possible task states:

- Available
- Assigned
- Accepted
- Active
- Completed
- Failed
- Expired
- Reassigned
- Emergency Override

Not every character must complete a rigid checklist every calendar day. The system should create **meaningful role-appropriate work** while preserving player freedom.

---

# 8. Duty Performance Affects Rank and Reputation

Doing the role well should matter.

Positive performance can improve:

- professional reputation
- community trust
- promotion eligibility
- voter support
- salary/contract opportunities where appropriate
- access to higher responsibility
- awards/recognition

Neglect or abuse can cause:

- complaints
- reduced reputation
- failed promotion
- disciplinary action
- loss of command
- election defeat
- recall/removal where allowed
- dismissal/suspension
- public scandal/news

A Police Captain who never manages the department should not remain respected merely because the rank was once earned.

A Mayor who lets services collapse may lose the next election.

A King who repeatedly fails the realm can lose community/faction support through whatever lawful removal/succession systems that government permits.

---

# 9. Duty Boards, Dispatch and Work Sources

Different role types can receive work differently.

Possible systems:

- police dispatch
- hospital patient queue
- construction contract board
- business work orders
- city public-contract board
- government agenda/dashboard
- faction command board
- emergency alerts
- NPC/player requests
- scheduled shift assignments
- Event Ledger-triggered responsibilities

The task system should reuse common underlying data rather than creating an unrelated quest framework for every profession.

Suggested task record:

- Task ID
- Role/Profession Tags
- Minimum Rank
- Jurisdiction
- Issuer
- Target/location
- Priority
- Start/expiry time
- Required capabilities/profession
- Payment/reward if applicable
- Reputation effects
- Failure consequences
- Evidence/Event Ledger links
- World-state trigger

# 11. Rank Does Not Mean Omnipotence

A higher roleplay rank does not automatically grant every capability.

A King may outrank a Police Captain politically, but the King does not automatically gain:

- Gunsmith knowledge
- Builder qualification
- Doctor abilities
- private bank access
- ownership of other players' property
- immunity from evidence
- immunity from hunger
- immunity from permanent death

Rank, profession, skills, ownership, legal authority and capabilities remain separate systems.

> Authority tells the world your legitimate role. It does not let you bypass the physical rules of the world.

---

# 12. Elections, Promotion & Community Trust

Political offices can require:

- eligibility
- community reputation
- citizenship/residency
- candidate registration
- campaign
- votes
- certification

Professional ranks can require:

- service
- training
- performance
- lawful conduct
- department trust
- community trust
- successful duty history
- appointment/promotion/vote where configured

GrimKoin cannot directly increase official Rank Tier.

Campaign spending, advertising, staff and events may be legitimate; bribery/corruption remains risky criminal gameplay rather than legitimate progression.

---

# 13. Death, Vacancy & Succession

If a King, President, Governor, Mayor, Police Captain or other officeholder permanently dies:

- that character loses the office because the character is dead
- active duties are reassigned/closed as appropriate
- the office becomes vacant or enters interim/succession state
- the player's new character does not automatically inherit the role
- government/organization rules select an interim or permanent replacement
- Event Ledger records major leadership death/vacancy
- Metaworld News may report it

A will can affect private property/business inheritance but does not automatically transfer elected/earned public office.

---

# 14. Core Hierarchy

Canonical in-world roleplay order:

```text
KING / SOVEREIGN QUEEN
      ↓
PRESIDENT
      ↓
GOVERNOR / REGIONAL LEADER
      ↓
MAYOR / CITY LEADER
      ↓
COUNCIL / SENIOR CIVIC OFFICIALS
      ↓
DEPARTMENT HEADS / CHIEFS / COMMISSIONERS
      ↓
PROFESSIONAL COMMAND RANKS
      ↓
PROFESSIONALS / OFFICERS / WORKERS
      ↓
CITIZENS / RESIDENTS
```

Not every government requires every layer, but when these ranks coexist this is their canonical relative placement.

Final rules:

> There is no Owner rank in Metaworld gameplay.

> The creator plays by the same in-world rules as every other player.

> King / Sovereign Queen is the highest playable world rank, followed by President.

> Every role carries responsibilities and world-driven duties fitting that role.

> Rank is something a character earns and maintains through service, trust and roleplay — not something bought with GrimKoin.
