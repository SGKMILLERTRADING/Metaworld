# Metaworld — Security Cameras, Evidence & Masked Identity

## Canonical Rule

Security cameras are common throughout Metaworld and can exist inside and outside stores, banks, bars, clubs, businesses, streets, parking lots, public buildings, apartment complexes, transport locations, and other appropriate places.

Cameras are part of the living-world evidence system. They do not magically reveal information that the camera could not actually see.

Core rule:

> The camera records the visible person, not the hidden account identity.

If a player commits a crime while wearing a mask that fully hides their face, the footage records a masked person. It does not automatically reveal the player's face, character name, account ID, or legal identity.

If the same crime is committed with the face clearly visible, the footage can preserve that face as evidence and may allow investigators or witnesses to identify the character if they know or later identify that face.

Possible camera locations include:

- Hotels
- Apartment lobbies
- Parking lots
- Gas stations

- Player businesses
- Police stations
- Hospitals
- City buildings
- Public streets/intersections where installed
- Transit stations
- Malls/markets
- Drive-ins/cinemas
- Property security systems
- Outdoor building entrances

Cameras can be city-owned, business-owned, property-owner-owned, bank-owned, police-operated, or part of another authorized security system.

---

# 2. What a Camera Records

A camera record can contain only information that the camera system was physically capable of observing at that time.

Possible recorded evidence:

- Timestamp
- Camera ID
- Location
- Visible face, if exposed and clear enough
- Mask/helmet/face covering
- Clothing
- Body build/silhouette
- Hair if visible
- Equipment or weapons visibly carried
- Actions performed
- Entry/exit direction
- Vehicle appearance
- Vehicle plate/identifier if visible
- Objects taken or dropped
- Other visible participants
- Lighting/weather/visibility conditions
- Audio only if that specific camera/security device supports recording audio under Metaworld rules

The camera does not automatically expose hidden account metadata.

---

# 3. Mask Rule — No Face, No Automatic Face Identification

If a face covering successfully blocks the face from the camera, the recorded evidence should show the covering instead of the hidden face.

Examples:

Camera sees:
- Black mask
- Dark jacket
- Blue pants
- Rifle
- Approximate body build
- Vehicle leaving scene

Camera does **not** magically append:
- Character Name: John Smith
- Account ID: 12345
- Hidden face image

This is the intended Metaworld rule.

A mask protects visual facial identity only. It does not erase every other piece of evidence in the world.

---

# 4. Unmasked Criminals

A criminal who commits a recorded crime with their face visible creates much stronger evidence.

A police officer reviewing footage may see a clear face.

If the officer, another witness, an NPC, a database, or another legitimate investigation system can connect that face to a known character, the suspect can be identified.

A careless criminal may therefore make police work dramatically easier simply by failing to conceal their face.

---

# 5. Police Investigation Gameplay

Police do not receive an automatic suspect name simply because a crime happened on camera.

A police player or NPC investigator can review evidence such as:

- Camera footage
- Witness statements
- Visible faces
- Masks/clothing
- Vehicle descriptions
- License plates where visible
- Entry/exit routes
- Stolen-item records
- Tracking codes where legally available
- Known associates
- Prior verified incidents
- Other server-verified evidence systems

The quality of the investigation depends on the quality of the available evidence.

Example:

Store robbery occurs.
-> Officer reviews camera.
-> Four masked suspects are visible.
-> No faces are visible.
-> Officer has a crime event and descriptions, but no automatic identities.
-> Investigation continues.

Different robbery:
-> Suspect enters without mask.
-> Camera records clear face.
-> Officer recognizes the person or later matches the face through legitimate in-world evidence.
-> Investigation has a strong named suspect.

---

# 6. Evidence Is Not Omniscience

Metaworld's Event Ledger can verify that a crime occurred without automatically revealing every offender's hidden identity to police or the news.

The Event Ledger may know internally which server actor performed an action for anti-cheat, moderation, rollback, and authoritative simulation purposes.

That private server truth is different from what ordinary characters are allowed to know in-world.

Core separation:

Server truth
-> knows authoritative actor IDs for technical integrity

In-world evidence
-> reveals only what witnesses, cameras, records, investigation, and legal systems legitimately expose

This prevents the gameplay world from becoming magically omniscient while keeping the server secure and authoritative.

---

# 7. News Uses Available Public Evidence

Metaworld News can use camera footage from verified incidents where the footage is legally/operationally available to the news system.

If suspects are masked, news may show/report masked suspects rather than reveal hidden identities.

Example:

> Police are searching for three masked suspects recorded during last night's downtown robbery.

If a face was captured and authorities release that evidence, news may show the face or identify the suspect according to the world rules.

News should never reveal a hidden character identity merely because the backend server knows which account controlled the actor.

---

# 8. Camera Ownership and Access

Not every player can freely watch every camera.

Access depends on ownership, employment, permissions, warrants/legal authority where required, and system rules.

Examples:

- Store owner can review their store cameras.
- Authorized store security can review them.
- Police may gain access through cooperation, emergency rules, warrants, or other legal gameplay systems.
- A random citizen cannot simply open the bank's camera network.

Camera access should be logged so misuse by public workers or employees can itself become an auditable event where appropriate.

---

# 9. Camera Reliability

Cameras should not be perfect magical witnesses.

Evidence quality can be affected by:

- Viewing angle
- Distance
- Lighting
- Weather
- Smoke/fire
- Obstructions
- Camera quality
- Damage
- Power loss
- Network/service failure
- Mask/helmet coverage
- Crowds

This creates situations where some footage is clear and other footage is incomplete.

---

# 10. Integration With Utilities and Security

Security cameras depend on actual world infrastructure.

A property owner may need:

- Electricity
- Camera equipment
- Recording/storage service
- Network/communications where required
- Maintenance
- Backup power if they want continued operation during outages

If power is cut and the property has no backup system, cameras may stop recording.

This ties security back into electricians, utility bills, generators, business costs, and property preparation.

---

# 11. Core Principle

Metaworld cameras create evidence, not omniscience.

If the camera sees a face, that face can become evidence.

If the camera sees a mask, the evidence is a masked person.

Police must investigate what the world actually revealed.

The backend server may know the true actor for technical authority, but police, NPCs, news, and players only receive identity information through legitimate in-world evidence.

This keeps crime risky, police work meaningful, masks useful, careless criminals punishable, and the world believable.
